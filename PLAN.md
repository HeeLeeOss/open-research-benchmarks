## Summary

Transparent, reproducible benchmark specifications for common open-science tasks, with clear datasets, metrics, and baseline results.

## Scope

In scope: benchmark task definitions, metrics, datasets (open), and reproducible baselines. Out of scope: benchmarks depending on closed data or that could enable misuse.

## Risk

Medium: built on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; educational/derivative in nature, no personal data, and no farm-/case-specific prescriptive advice. Each deliverable must cite sources and stay within established, verifiable material.

## Approach

Each task defines one benchmark: the task, open dataset, metric, run protocol, and a baseline result.

## Definition of shipped

Documented, reproducible benchmark specifications for science tasks with baselines, published openly and reused by a research group.
